# Aura Journal - Data Deletion Instructions

## How to Delete Your Account and Data

If you wish to delete your Aura Journal account and all associated data, you have two options:

### Option 1: Delete from the App

1. Open the **Aura Journal** app
2. Go to **Settings** (tap the gear icon)
3. Scroll down and tap **"Delete Account"**
4. Confirm your decision
5. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted within 30 days

### Option 2: Email Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion via email:

1. Send an email to: **alfieopo@gmail.com**
2. Use the subject line: **"Aura Journal - Account Deletion Request"**
3. Include in your email:
   - The email address associated with your account
   - Confirmation that you want to delete all your data
4. We will process your request within 7 business days
5. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete

## What Data Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

- ✅ Your account information (email, name, profile)
- ✅ All journal entries (voice recordings and transcriptions)
- ✅ Astrological profile and preferences
- ✅ Generated horoscopes and insights
- ✅ App settings and preferences
- ✅ Authentication tokens and session data

## Data Deletion Timeline

- **Immediate:** Your account is deactivated and you can no longer access the app
- **Within 30 days:** All your data is permanently deleted from our servers
- **Backups:** Data in backups will be deleted within 90 days

## Cannot Be Recovered

⚠️ **Warning:** Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. Please make sure to export any journal entries you want to keep before deleting your account.
